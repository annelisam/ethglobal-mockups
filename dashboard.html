What this mockup shows

Hacker: the logged-in dashboard for one event. A status stepper and checklist make every step obvious, then tabs for Referrals (auto-generated code + link, click/conversion stats, tiered rewards, leaderboard, social card generator) and Team finding (looking-for-team toggle, skill matches, idea board). The ETHGlobal builder profile is a separate product and lives outside this event dashboard.

Community: the cohort dashboard for group sign-ups (a university club here), with a shared code, member roster, milestone rewards modeled on the ETHConf Community Partners tiers, and a community profile map.

All names, numbers, reward tiers and matches are sample data. The persona (Ana Ferreira) is fictional. Reward specifics need locking with the team before anything ships.
