About the hackathon

A weekend where prototypes become products

ETHGlobal Lisbon puts you in a room with the most obsessive builders in the ecosystem for 36 hours. You arrive with a laptop. You leave with a working project, a team you built it with, and feedback from the engineers behind the protocols you built on.

It's free. Accepted hackers pay nothing. Food, drinks, WiFi and workspace are covered from Friday check-in to Sunday's closing ceremony. A small refundable stake confirms your spot.

It's beginner friendly. 29+ workshops, hands-on mentors from 10+ protocol teams, and judges who care more about what you learned than what you knew walking in.

It compounds. Projects from past ETHGlobal weekends have raised funding, found users and become part of the core Ethereum stack. The weekend is the start, not the whole story.

New for 2026 · Continuity

Start over. Or continue building.

For the first time, your hackathon project doesn't have to start at zero. Pick the path that fits where you are, and build with up to three partner SDKs in a team of 1–5.

PATH 01

Classic

Empty repo, blank canvas, 36 hours. The format that started it all: type git init on Friday and demo something real on Sunday.

PATH 02

Extend open source

Bring a repo you already maintain and finally ship the feature you've been putting off. Judges score the delta you build over the weekend.

PATH 03

Ship a feature

Build one new feature on an existing product and release it as open source. Ship to real users before the closing ceremony.

Keep building after Sunday with Spotlight. The strongest Continuity projects are followed after the event: continued check-ins with mentors, visibility to partners who fund what they saw at the demo, and a stage for progress updates. The hackathon is where it starts, Spotlight is how it keeps going.

FAQ

Quick answers

The five most-asked questions. The full FAQ lives on its own page, where every answer can rank on its own.

Is ETHGlobal Lisbon free to attend? ▾
Yes. It's free for accepted hackers, with food, drinks, WiFi and workspace covered all weekend. A small refundable stake confirms your spot after acceptance, and you get it back when you show up and submit.
Do I need a team or an idea before applying? ▾
No. Apply solo and form a team of up to 5 on site. Plenty of hackers arrive without an idea and find one in the first workshops.
Does my project have to start from scratch? ▾
Not anymore. New for 2026, you can build from an empty repo the classic way, extend an open-source repo you already maintain, or ship one new open-source feature on an existing product.
I'm a beginner. Should I still apply? ▾
Yes. The event is beginner friendly by design: 29+ workshops, hands-on mentors from partner teams, and judging that rewards what you learned and shipped over the weekend.
Is Pragma Lisbon included in my hacker ticket? ▾
No, Pragma is a separate, ticketed one-day summit at the same venue on July 25. Confirmed hackers get a discounted $49 ticket.
